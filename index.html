About

Who is writing this

Twenty-six years between the drawing board and the compiler.

I started in physics at the Universidad Nacional de Colombia, moved into graphic design at FADU/UBA, and have spent most of my working life on the frontend: TV streaming platforms, cloud point of sale, retail AI, and today the frontend team at Deel.

I care about the layer where design meets code. At Intuitivo I built a token-based design system from scratch; on my own time I build tools that blend mathematics with interactive visuals, from pitch detection to point clouds.

I also photograph, illustrate, and write The Polymath Manifesto, an essay series about the structural connections between mathematics, philosophy, design and culture.
