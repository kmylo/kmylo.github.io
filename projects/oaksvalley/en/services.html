How can I find out more about the company?

To learn more about our proposals and trajectory, please go to the Contact section, where you can send us your query or learn about our lines of communication. We await your message!

Who has control of my funds?

Your funds are at all times under your own control. Oaks Valley is only authorized to carry out operations on your account, but never to withdraw or deposit funds in it. Your money, your control.

How can we start a business relationship?

To start a business relationship, you can contact us by phone, via email or by completing the form in the Contact section. You will be able to talk with an account executive to align your objectives with our services.

Can I hire your services without knowing about finances?

Certainly. We are pleased to provide information that potentially helps create greater financial intelligence for our clients. Contact us and an agent will be able to answer all your doubts before starting to do business together.

What entities regulate the custodian of my account?

All investment accounts are held by a custodian under the observation of the U.S. Securities and Exchanges Commission (SEC) and the Financial Industry Regulatory Authority (FINRA), guaranteeing the transparency of the most regulated market in the world.
